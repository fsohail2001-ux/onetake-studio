OneTake Studio

A desktop app that turns gaming stream highlights into short vertical clips and publishes them, on a schedule, to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and X accounts.

Record & detect

Records streams and uploaded videos locally and finds the best moments.

Clip & render

Cuts moments into 9:16 clips and renders them on the user's own computer.

Schedule & post

Posts finished clips to the accounts the user connects, at the time the user chooses.

How it works

OneTake Studio runs entirely on the user's own Mac. The user signs in to each platform with that platform's official login and chooses what to share. OneTake Studio only uploads videos the user has created and approved, and only to the user's own accounts. It does not read, collect or sell anyone else's data.
