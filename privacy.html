Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OneTake Studio ("OneTake", "we", "us") handles information when you use the OneTake Studio desktop application (the "App"). OneTake Studio is a personal tool that runs on your own computer and publishes short videos you create to social media accounts you connect.

1. The short version

  • The App runs locally on your computer. We do not operate servers that receive your videos or account data.
  • We only access the platform data needed to publish videos you choose to your own accounts.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can disconnect any account and delete all stored data at any time.

2. Information the App accesses

When you connect a social media account, you sign in through that platform's official login screen and approve specific permissions. Depending on the platform, the App may receive:

  • Account identifiers: your account or channel ID, display name, username and profile picture, used to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: credentials issued by the platform that let the App act on your behalf within the permissions you granted.
  • Publishing information: the status and ID of posts the App publishes for you, so it can show whether a post succeeded.

The App also stores the videos, recordings, captions, schedules and settings you create in it. These stay in your library folder on your computer.

3. How the information is used

Information is used only to provide the App's features you use: connecting your accounts, uploading and publishing videos you have chosen, scheduling posts at the times you set, and showing the result. We do not use it for any other purpose.

4. Where information is stored

All information is stored on your own computer. Access tokens and app credentials are encrypted (AES-256) with a key kept in your operating system's secure keychain. Nothing is sent to us. Data is sent only to the platform you are publishing to (for example, a video you schedule for YouTube is uploaded to YouTube).

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone. Information leaves your computer only when the App sends your content to the platform you selected, at your direction.

6. Platform-specific information

YouTube and Google. The App uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. OneTake Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke the App's access at any time from your Google security settings.

TikTok. The App uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API to publish videos you choose to your TikTok account. You can remove access from TikTok's settings under Security → Manage app permissions.

Instagram and Facebook (Meta). The App uses Facebook Login for Business to publish Reels to the Instagram professional account and Facebook Page you select. You can remove access from Facebook Settings → Business Integrations. See also the Meta Privacy Policy.

X. The App uses the X API to publish posts you choose to your X account. You can revoke access in X under Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information is kept on your computer until you delete it. To delete it, disconnect the account in the App (which deletes its stored tokens), revoke access on the platform as described above, and delete the App's library and configuration folders. Because we hold no copy, nothing remains with us.

Data deletion requests: you can also email fsohail2001@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and we will explain how to remove everything. We will reply within 30 days.

8. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly process information about children.

9. Security

We protect stored credentials with encryption and keep data on your own device. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your computer protected with a password and up-to-date software.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: fsohail2001@gmail.com.
